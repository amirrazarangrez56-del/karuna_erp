--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DAILY MORNING ROUTINE (STAFF STARTUP - ZERO CONFUSION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: Turn on the Network Switch / Router Box (ensure power LED is ON).
STEP 2: Turn on Computer 1 (Owner PC) ➔ Double-click 'Karuna Hotel POS (Owner)' on Desktop.
STEP 3: Turn on Laptop 2 & Laptop 3 ➔ Double-click 'Karuna Hotel POS (Billing)' on Desktop.

That's it! In 5 seconds, all billing counters are live and connected.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DAILY NIGHT ROUTINE (CLOSING SHIFT & OWNER REPORT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. On Computer 1, click "Settle Bills" (बिल तपासणी) or "Owner Admin" (मालक पॅनेल).
2. View:
   • Total Daily Revenue (एकूण विक्री)
   • Counter 1 Total vs Counter 2 Total vs Counter 3 Total
   • Cash collected in cash drawer vs Online / UPI collections vs Card
3. Click "Offline Local Backup" ➔ "Export Full Backup" or "Export Food Items (CSV)" to save daily ledger.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. TOP 5 REAL-WORLD PROBLEMS & INSTANT SOLUTIONS (TROUBLESHOOTING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PROBLEM 1: "Laptop says 'Retrying connection / Disconnected' in morning"
SOLUTION:
  a. Ensure Computer 1 (Owner PC) is turned on first and START_OWNER_DESKTOP_SERVER.bat is running.
  b. Run 'ALLOW_FIREWALL_ON_SERVER.bat' once on Computer 1 to allow LAN connections.
  c. Check if the LAN wire is plugged into both the laptop and the switch (green/orange blinking light).

PROBLEM 2: "Staff changed or Cashier shifted counters"
SOLUTION:
  • In top-right header, click the counter badge (e.g. Counter 1 ➔ Switch to Counter 2).

PROBLEM 3: "Need to add a new dish, change price, or add multiple prices (e.g. 250g, 500g, 1 Kg)"
SOLUTION:
  • On Computer 1, go to Owner Admin ➔ 'Dishes & Menu Management'.
  • Add or edit price ➔ Click Save.

PROBLEM 4: "Need offline safety backup"
SOLUTION:
  • Go to Owner Admin ➔ 'Offline Local Backup' ➔ Click 'Download Backup File (.json)'.
